Jerry

Dad · Hack inventor · Bucket hat enthusiast

Every hack on this channel comes from the same guy: Jerry, camo bucket hat permanently attached, delivering each "genius" fix like he's the first person to ever think of it. He hasn't tested most of these. That's not the point.

The premise

Dad Hacks 'N Facts HQ is short-form comedy built around one very specific character trait: absolute, unshakeable confidence in solutions nobody asked for. A bar of soap frees a stuck zipper. A bag of frozen peas fixes what frozen peas fix. A leaf blower turns into a charcoal starter. Jerry explains it straight to camera like he's on an infomercial, and the joke is that he half-believes it.

Why this exists

Because "dad energy" — the specific mix of confidence, improvisation, and mild danger that comes with fixing something the wrong way on purpose — is funny, and there wasn't enough of it in short-form video. So: a hat, a garage, and a new hack every few days.

Where to find him

New hacks land on YouTube Shorts. Subscribe if any of this sounds useful, which it probably isn't.
