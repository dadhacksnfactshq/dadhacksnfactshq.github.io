Dad Hacks 'N Facts HQ

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 29, 2026

Dad Hacks 'N Facts HQ ("we," "us") produces short-form comedy videos and publishes them to YouTube. This page explains what this application does and what data it accesses.

What this application does

This is a personal-use application built to publish videos to the Dad Hacks 'N Facts HQ YouTube channel on behalf of its owner. It uses the YouTube Data API solely to upload video files, titles, descriptions, and tags to that channel.

What data we access

The application requests the youtube.upload permission scope from Google. This permission is used only to upload videos to the Dad Hacks 'N Facts HQ channel. It does not read, collect, or share any other data from your Google account, and it is not used to access any account other than the one authorizing it.

Data from this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind, and does not collect any personal information from visitors.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. Google's own privacy policy governs how Google handles authorization data; see Google's Privacy Policy.
